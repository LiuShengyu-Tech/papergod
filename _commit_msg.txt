fix: embedded quotes followed by lowercase no longer split sentences

A quoted question or exclamation embedded mid-sentence keeps the sentence
going with a lowercase word (e.g. ..."Can machines think?" is to be sought...),
but the splitter treated the punctuation as a boundary whenever only
whitespace followed. Add a rule: after .?! (and any closing quotes) the next
non-space character must NOT be lowercase for a boundary to count; lowercase
means the main clause continues.

- sentenceRanges and splitSentences both skip the split when the follower is
  lowercase (embedded quote continues)
- Turing example re-joins 10 mid-sentence quoted clauses (625 to 615 sentences)
- 120 tests pass, including a new embedded-quote regression case
